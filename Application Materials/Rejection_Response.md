# Rejection / Status-Update Response Template

Reference tone/format for replying to a rejection, interview invite, or other status
update, per `interview-prep.md`'s "Thank-You / Response Notes" section. Keep it short
(2-3 sentences), warm, and brief — this is not the place to re-pitch yourself.

## Rejection response

> Thank you for letting me know, and for taking the time to consider my application.
> I really enjoyed learning more about [Company/team] through the process, and I'd
> welcome the chance to be considered for future roles that might be a fit. Wishing
> you and the team continued success.

## Interview-invite acknowledgment

> Thank you for reaching out — I'm glad to move forward and would be happy to schedule
> a time that works. [Any scheduling constraints, if relevant.] Looking forward to
> speaking with you.

Adjust tone/specifics per your own voice once you've sent a few of these for real.
