# Cover Letter Sample (structural reference)

This is a placeholder. Once you've written and sent a real cover letter you're happy
with, replace this file's content with that real letter — it becomes the style/tone
reference `cover-letter-templates.md` points to for every future cover letter.

Until then, here's the structural skeleton to start from (see
`.claude/skills/application-assistant/cover-letter-templates.md` for the full guide):

---

[Your Name]
[email@example.com] | [phone] | [LinkedIn URL]

[Date]

Dear [Hiring Manager Name / Company Hiring Team],

I am writing to express my interest in the [Role] position at [Company]. [1 sentence
connecting your background to this posting.]

Here's why I believe I am a great fit for this position:

**[Category Label 1, drawn from the posting's own priorities]:** [1-2 sentences
connecting a specific experience/skill to this requirement.]

**[Category Label 2]:** [1-2 sentences.]

**[Category Label 3]:** [1-2 sentences.]

Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to discussing how [1 forward-looking
sentence tying your background to what the employer needs].

Best,
[Your Name]

---

Word budget: 250-300 words of body text, 1 page hard limit. No em-dashes. Every claim
backed by something real. See `writing-style.md` and `cover-letter-templates.md` for
the full rules.
